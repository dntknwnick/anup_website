About the academy · Vijayanagar, Bengaluru

Learn industrial automation by building with it.

Our academy helps learners build practical, job-ready skills in industrial automation, robotics and machine vision.

Our mission

Make automation skills practical and accessible.

To make industrial automation skills accessible, practical and job-ready for every learner who wants a career in robotics.

Our vision

Prepare engineers to contribute from day one.

To be the academy employers trust first when they look for automation engineers who can contribute from day one.

How we teach

Bench-first, practical learning.

The programme connects control logic, electrical systems, robotics and machine vision through hands-on exercises. You spend most of your time at the bench, learning to apply each concept on real equipment.

Four connected modules lead to a capstone: design, program and commission an automated work cell, then present it to a panel.
