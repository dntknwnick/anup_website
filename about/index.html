About

A service company that also trains the next automation engineer.

Qusaraengg exists to deliver industrial robotics and PLC work for factories, and to train students on the same practical skills used on those jobs.

How we work

We do not separate “training world” from “factory world”. Students learn tools that our service team uses on real machines. Industry clients get engineers who can wire, program and commission — not only explain a slide.

  • Robotics and PLC service jobs for plants
  • Control panel, installation and commissioning support
  • Student and professional training batches
  • Corporate upskilling on your shop floor
