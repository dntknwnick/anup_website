Privacy

When you contact the academy through the admissions form, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, optional email address, learner type, programme interest and optional message.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry about the programme, discuss your learning goals and arrange a lab visit. The form details are also held temporarily in this browser tab's session storage to prepare the WhatsApp message and its retry link; session storage is cleared when the tab is closed. If you contact us through WhatsApp or by phone, the details you share there are handled through those channels.
