Programme FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

What will I learn?

The programme covers PLC and HMI, electrical and automation, industrial robotics, and AI and machine vision.

Who can join?

The programme is for students, working professionals, diploma holders, engineering students and career switchers.

How long is the programme?

It is a 3-month hands-on programme, with most learning time spent at the bench.

Will I work on real equipment?

Yes. Training takes place with real PLCs, robots, drives and machine vision at the Vijayanagar lab.

What is the capstone?

Design, program and commission an automated work cell, then present it to a panel.

Is there placement support?

The programme includes 100% Dedicated Placement Assistance. A placement team stays with you from your first lesson to your first interview.

How do I enquire?

Use the admissions form, WhatsApp or phone. The team will call back to discuss the programme and arrange a lab visit.
