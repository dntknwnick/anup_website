Training FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

How long is the Industrial Automation & Robotics programme?

The programme is structured over 3 months and covers PLC and HMI, electrical automation, industrial robotics, and AI and machine vision.

What topics are covered in the training?

Topics include PLC programming and ladder logic, HMI, electrical wiring, sensors, drives, VFDs, robot programming, robot integration and machine-vision fundamentals.

Which PLC brands are included in practice?

Training PLC brands include Siemens, Mitsubishi, Omron and Delta.

Is this course suitable if I am new to industrial automation?

Contact the admissions team to discuss your background and whether the programme is a good fit for your current experience and goals.
