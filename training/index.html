Training FAQ

What is the difference between the two courses?

Course 1 is 2 months of industrial automation: PLC, wiring, sensors, HMI, VFD and projects. Course 2 is 3 months and adds advanced PLC, robots, PLC–robot integration, machine vision and an integrated automation project.

Which PLC brands will I work on?

Partner PLCs used in training include Siemens, Mitsubishi, Omron and Delta.

Do I need prior PLC experience?

No for Course 1. Course 2 suits learners who want robotics and advanced integration after the automation basics.
