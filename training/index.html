The 3-month programme

Four stages. One working automated cell.

Each module builds on the one before it, from wiring a PLC to teaching a robot to see. You spend most of your time at the bench, not in a lecture hall.

01

CONTROL SYSTEMS

PLC & HMI

  • PLC hardware and wiring
  • Ladder logic
  • Timers, counters and interlocks
  • Digital and analog I/O
  • HMI design
  • SCADA and industrial communication

Outcome: Build a foundation in programmable control and operator interfaces.

Labs used: PLC & HMI Lab

Electrical & Automation

  • VFDs and pneumatics
  • Motion control
  • Safety and troubleshooting

Outcome: Understand the electrical and control components used in automated equipment.
